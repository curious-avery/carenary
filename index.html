Why I built Carenery

A doctor in the house.
A guide for every family.

Carenery began with a simple question: How can families find health information they can trust?

Developer

Avery L.

My dad is a doctor. When an online health search got confusing, I could ask him which source to trust.

I saw young friends search for answers about a parent or grandparent. They did not always have a doctor at home to help. Even my dad is only one person, and no one can know every rare or long-term condition.

I built Carenery to feel like a trusted doctor in the family: a clear place to start, a guide to good sources, and help getting ready to talk with a real care team.

01

Start with trust

Use public health sources and show where each fact comes from.

02

Use clear words

Make health information easier for families to read and discuss.

Support real care
